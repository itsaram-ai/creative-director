# TikTok platform notes

TikTok distribution is interest-graph driven, not follower driven: every
post is tested on a fresh audience slice, so consistency of niche matters
more than follower count. Watch time and completion rate are the dominant
ranking signals; rewatches are gold.

Native voice: fast cuts, on-screen text, trending audio. Text posts and
carousels now exist but video remains dominant. Ideal text-post length is
under 100 words with a strong first line.

Hashtags on TikTok function as topic classifiers for the algorithm, not
discovery tools for users. 3-5 specific tags outperform broad tags like
#fyp, which add no classification signal.

Posting time matters less than on other platforms because of the testing
model, but the first hour's engagement velocity still influences the first
distribution wave.
