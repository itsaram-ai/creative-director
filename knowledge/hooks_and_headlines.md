# Hooks and headlines

The first line of any short-form post decides 80% of its performance. A hook
must create an information gap: the reader learns just enough to need the
rest. Questions underperform statements on most platforms because they are
easy to answer internally and scroll past.

Specificity beats cleverness. "I grew a newsletter to 12,000 readers with
one weekly email" outperforms "How I grew my newsletter fast". Numbers,
named tools, and concrete outcomes signal that the payoff is real.

Negative framing ("stop doing X") reliably outperforms positive framing
("start doing Y") for advice content, but overuse erodes trust. Alternate
framings across a content calendar.

The hook and the payoff must match. Clickbait that overpromises trains the
audience to ignore future posts; platforms measure this as poor retention
and suppress reach accordingly.
