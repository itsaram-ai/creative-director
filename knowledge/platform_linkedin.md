# LinkedIn platform notes

LinkedIn rewards dwell time: posts that make readers pause, expand "see
more", and read to the end. The first two lines appear above the fold and
function as the hook; a line break after a strong first sentence increases
expand rates.

Personal narrative outperforms corporate announcement by a wide margin.
"What I learned failing at X" consistently beats "Our company is excited
to announce". First-person, specific, slightly vulnerable.

Engagement in the first 90 minutes heavily influences reach. Replying to
every early comment extends distribution; posts that spark comment threads
between readers perform best of all.

Hashtags matter little on LinkedIn; 3 specific ones suffice. External links
in the post body suppress reach - put links in the first comment instead.
