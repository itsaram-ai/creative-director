# Writing for Gen Z audiences

Gen Z audiences (born roughly 1997-2012) reward authenticity signals and
punish polish that reads as corporate. Lowercase styling, self-aware humour,
and admitting limitations all increase trust. Overproduced visuals and
marketing language ("game-changing", "revolutionary") trigger immediate
scroll-past.

Attention is earned in the first 1.5 seconds on video platforms and the
first line of text posts. Front-load the most surprising element; never
warm up.

Participation beats broadcasting. Content framed as a conversation starter
("tell me I'm wrong", open questions at the end, duet/stitch bait)
significantly outperforms one-way announcements.

Gen Z fact-checks. Claims without sources get called out in comments, and
comment sentiment drives the algorithm. Cite specifics or hedge honestly.
